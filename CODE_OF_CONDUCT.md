# Code of Conduct

CodeTutor is a learning project. Contributors must create a welcoming environment for
beginners, children, educators, experienced developers, and people from every background.

Be patient, specific, and respectful. Do not shame questions, mistakes, reading level,
experience, language ability, or learning speed. Critique ideas and code without attacking
people. Do not publish another person's code, learner profile, credentials, or personal
information.

Harassment, discriminatory language, sexualized conduct, threats, deliberate intimidation,
and disclosure of private information are unacceptable. Maintainers may remove content or
participation that violates these expectations. A private enforcement contact should be
added before the first public community release.
